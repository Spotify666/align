# 06 · Content and voice

## Principles

- **Plain and specific.** "Your head finished behind your front knee at contact", not "suboptimal head position".
- **Shot words, not generic words.** Say "front-foot defence" or "shot". Use "delivery" only for the ball bowled.
- **Honest about limits.** "Estimate", "not measured", "uncalibrated" and "provisional" are always visible where they apply.
- **One thing to train.** One priority, at most two drills, each with a dosage and a pass condition.
- **No medical language.** Never "injury", "diagnosis", "biomarker" or "risk". The report validator blocks these.

## Status copy

| Status | Headline pattern |
|---|---|
| Valid | "Valid front-foot defence. Main priority: …" |
| Different shot | "This appears to be a pull shot, not a front-foot defence." |
| Uncertain | "We can't confirm a front-foot defence: … " + what to change |
| Capture failed | "We couldn't use this recording: …" + fixes |

## Numbers

Show the value, unit and ± uncertainty, rounded to the metric's stated precision. Every number in generated text must match the payload at that precision and cite its evidence id.

## Labels

- Fixtures: **DEMO DATA** on every screen and in every PDF.
- Buttons: verbs ("Analyse front-foot defence", "Download PDF", "Zoom to batter").
